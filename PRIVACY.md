# Privacy Policy

This application is for personal use by Mads Tjalve only.

It accesses my own Oura data with my explicit authorization
to download and analyse sleep, activity, workouts, stress,
and other available health metrics.

Downloaded data and access credentials are stored privately
on my own computer, never in this public repository.

The application does not sell data or automatically share
it with third parties. I may choose to upload selected data
to ChatGPT for analysis, subject to ChatGPT's privacy policy.

I retain downloaded data until I delete it. I can revoke
the application's access through my Oura account and delete
local data and credentials at any time.

No other users' data is collected.
